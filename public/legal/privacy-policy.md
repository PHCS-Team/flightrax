# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 23 September 2026
**Last updated:** 23 September 2026

This policy explains what personal information **FlightraX** collects, why, and
how it is handled. It applies to everyone who holds a FlightraX account.

FlightraX is developed and operated by the **FlightraX Team**. It is provided to
the flight training organisation that uses it to run its operations ("the
flight school").

Under the **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)**, the flight
school is the **personal information controller** — it decides who holds an
account and what happens to the operational records. The FlightraX Team is the
**personal information processor**, handling that information on the flight
school's behalf and only for the purposes described here.

## 1. Information We Collect

### When you register

- your **full name** and **email address**;
- the **role** you are applying for (student or instructor);
- your **identification number** issued by the flight school or by government;
- a **photograph of your identification document** (JPG, PNG, or WebP, up to
  5 MB).

### When you complete your profile

- your **profile photograph**;
- your **handwritten signature**, drawn in the app;
- a **security passcode**, used to confirm sensitive flight actions. We store
  only a cryptographic hash of it, never the passcode itself.

### Your licences and certificates

- **licence** type, number, rating, and expiry date, with images of the front
  and back;
- **certificate** title, description, and expiry date, with up to five images
  per certificate.

### Records created as you use the system

- **flight plans** you file, which include your name, your signature, and a copy
  of your licence details at the time of filing;
- **weight and balance sheets**, including the names and signatures of whoever
  prepared and verified them;
- **flight requests**, including who submitted, approved, or rejected them, when,
  and any rejection reason;
- **flights**, including who commenced, terminated, or cancelled them and when;
- **schedule entries** naming the assigned pilot and instructor;
- **flight logs** built from those records;
- **notifications** sent to you, which may contain other people's names.

### Technical information

- a **session cookie** that keeps you signed in;
- if you turn on push notifications, a **push subscription** for your device,
  including your browser's push address and browser identification string.

We do **not** use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools of any kind, and we
do not profile you or make automated decisions about you.

## 2. Why We Collect It

| Purpose | Information used |
|---|---|
| Verify you are affiliated with the flight school and approve your account | Name, email, ID number, ID document |
| Confirm your licences and certificates are valid before a flight | Licence and certificate records |
| Plan, authorise, and record flight operations | Flight plans, weight and balance, requests, flights |
| Keep the operational and training records the flight school must maintain | All of the above, plus the record of who did what |
| Confirm that a sensitive action was really taken by you | Security passcode |
| Notify you about flights, schedules, and account decisions | Name, email, push subscription |
| Keep your account secure | Password, session cookie |

Our lawful basis is the contract and enrolment relationship between you and the
flight school, the flight school's legal obligations as a training
organisation, and its legitimate interest in safe, traceable flight operations.

## 3. Who Can See Your Information

Access depends on your role.

- **Students** see their own records and the flights they are part of.
- **Instructors** additionally see their students' records, including licence
  and certificate details. Instructors cannot see one another's licence or
  certificate details.
- **Administrative personnel** see what their department requires: Flight
  Operations reviews accounts, credentials, and the schedule; Air Traffic
  Control views flight plans; Safety manages NOTAMs.

These limits are enforced in the database itself, not only in the screens you
see.

## 4. Information Visible to the Public

Two parts of FlightraX are visible to people who are not signed in. Please read
this section carefully.

### The flight monitor

FlightraX publishes a live flight monitor board, intended for display on a
screen in the lobby. It is reachable by anyone on the internet without signing
in, and for each flight that day it shows:

- the **student's name** and the **instructor's name**, shortened on screen to
  a first initial and surname (for example, "K. Bautista");
- the aircraft registration, destination, and departure and arrival times;
- current NOTAMs.

The data feed behind that board returns the **full, unshortened names**.

If you fly, your surname and your movements for that day are therefore public
information. Raise any concern about this with the flight school's Flight
Operations office.

### Profile photographs

Profile photographs and aircraft photographs are stored in public storage.
Anyone who has the direct link to an image can open it without signing in.
Please keep that in mind when choosing a profile photograph.

Your ID document, licence images, and certificate images are **not** public.
They are kept in private storage and are opened through temporary links that
expire — ten minutes for an ID document, one hour for licence, certificate, and
schedule files.

## 5. Who Else Handles Your Information

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for marketing.

It is handled by these service providers on our behalf:

- **Supabase** — the database, sign-in system, and file storage behind
  FlightraX. Everything you enter is stored there.
- **The email provider configured for FlightraX** — sends account emails such
  as password resets.
- **Your device's push service** — if you enable push notifications, the
  notification's title and text, which may include a person's name, passes
  through Google, Apple, or Mozilla's push service to reach your device. Turn
  push notifications off if you would rather this did not happen.
- **Our hosting provider** — runs the application.

Information may also be disclosed to the **Civil Aviation Authority of the
Philippines** or another authority where the flight school is required to
produce its operational or training records, and where disclosure is otherwise
required by law.

## 6. How Long We Keep It

Your account information is kept while your account exists.

**Flight records are permanent.** Flight plans, weight and balance sheets,
flight requests, flights, and the record of who did what are operational and
training records. They are not deleted when an account is closed, and the names
and signatures captured on a filed document stay on that document — that is
what makes it a reliable record.

Because those records reference your account, an account that has taken part in
any flight cannot simply be erased.

## 7. Keeping Your Information Secure

- Every table in the database enforces per-user access rules, so a request for
  someone else's record is refused at the database, not just hidden in the app.
- Uploaded ID, licence, and certificate images are stored privately and served
  through temporary links that expire.
- Your password is hashed by the sign-in system and never stored in readable
  form. Your security passcode is separately hashed and also never stored in
  readable form.
- Sensitive flight actions — commencing, terminating, cancelling, approving,
  and rejecting — require your passcode.
- Passwords must be at least 8 characters. Choose something long and unique to
  this system.

No system is completely secure. Keep your password and passcode private, and
report anything unusual to the flight school.

## 8. Your Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 you have the right to:

- **be informed** about how your information is collected and used;
- **access** the information held about you;
- **correct** information that is inaccurate or out of date;
- **object** to certain processing;
- **request erasure or blocking** of information that is unlawfully obtained,
  no longer necessary, or used beyond what you were told;
- **obtain a copy** of information you provided, in a usable format;
- **claim damages** for a violation of your rights;
- **complain to the National Privacy Commission**.

You can update much of your profile yourself from your account page. For
anything else, including a request for a copy of your information, contact the
flight school's Flight Operations office.

Please note that the right to erasure is not absolute. Where information forms
part of an operational or training record the flight school is required to keep,
or is needed to establish or defend a legal claim, it will be retained and we
will explain why.

## 9. Children

FlightraX is used by student pilots, some of whom may be under 18. If you are
under 18, register only with the consent of your parent or legal guardian. A
parent or guardian may contact the flight school about their child's
information.

## 10. Cookies

FlightraX uses one kind of cookie: the session cookie that keeps you signed in.
It is necessary for the system to work and is not used for tracking or
advertising. Clearing it signs you out.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The current version is always the one published on
this page, and the date at the top shows when it last changed. Material changes
will be announced within the system.

## 12. Contact

For your information, your records, or to exercise any of the rights above,
contact the flight school's Flight Operations office.

For questions about the system itself, contact the FlightraX Team.

You may also complain to the National Privacy Commission at
[privacy.gov.ph](https://privacy.gov.ph).
